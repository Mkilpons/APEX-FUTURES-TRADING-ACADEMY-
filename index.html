THE FOUNDATION

Five concepts every futures trader must internalize before risking a single dollar.

01

Market Structure

Price moves in trends. Identify Higher Highs and Higher Lows for bullish structure. This is your compass before every trade.

Beginner
02

Support & Resistance

Key price levels where buyers and sellers historically clash. Mark them before the session opens every single day.

Beginner
03

Pullback Entries

Never chase price. Wait for it to pull back to a key level. This gives you the best entry and the tightest stop.

Intermediate
04

Risk / Reward

Define your stop and target before every trade. A 1:2 R/R means you only need to be right 34% of the time to break even.

Essential
05

VWAP

Volume Weighted Average Price is the most watched intraday level by institutions. Price above = bullish. Below = bearish.
