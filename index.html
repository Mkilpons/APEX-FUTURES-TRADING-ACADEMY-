THE FOUNDATION

Five concepts every futures trader must internalize before risking a single dollar.

01

Market Structure

Price moves in trends. Identify Higher Highs and Higher Lows for bullish structure, Lower Highs and Lower Lows for bearish. This is your compass.

Beginner
02

Support & Resistance

Key price levels where buyers and sellers historically clash. Trades at these zones have asymmetric risk/reward. Mark them before the session opens.

Beginner
03

Pullback Entries

Never chase price. Wait for it to pull back to a key level within the trend. This gives you the best entry, tightest stop, and most room to profit.

Intermediate
04

Risk / Reward

Define your stop and target before every trade. A 1:2 R/R means you only need to be right 34% of the time to break even. Math is your edge.

Essential
05

VWAP

Volume Weighted Average Price is the most watched intraday level by institutions. Price above VWAP signals bullish bias. Below signals bearish.

Intermediate
