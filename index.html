THE FOUNDATION

Five concepts every futures trader must internalize before risking a single dollar.

01

Market Structure

Identifying Higher Highs and Higher Lows defines an uptrend. Lower Highs and Lower Lows define a downtrend. A Break of Structure (BOS) confirms trend continuation. A Change of Character (CHOCH) signals a potential reversal. This is the lens through which every trade decision is made.

Foundation
02

Support & Resistance

Key price levels where buyers and sellers have historically clashed. These are not single lines but zones of interest. Mark them from the higher time frame before the session opens — they act as magnets for price and define your trade boundaries.

Beginner
03

Liquidity Areas

Liquidity pools are price levels where large clusters of stop losses sit — above swing highs and below swing lows. Institutions deliberately sweep these levels to fill their orders before reversing. Equal highs, equal lows, and prior session extremes are prime liquidity targets. Don't place your stops in obvious locations.

Intermediate
04

Pullback Entries

Never chase price. After a move in your direction, wait for price to pull back to a key level — a support zone, order block, or fair value gap. This gives you a tighter stop, better risk/reward, and confirmation that the trend is still valid before committing to a trade.

Intermediate
05

Risk / Reward

Define your stop loss and profit target before every single trade — no exceptions. A minimum 1:2 risk/reward means you only need to win 34% of your trades to be profitable. A 1:3 ratio means you can lose 70% of the time and still break even. Math is your edge — protect it at all costs.
