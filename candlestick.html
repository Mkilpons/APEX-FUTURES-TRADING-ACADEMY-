// lesson 01 · anatomy
OPEN · HIGH · LOW · CLOSE

Every candle tells a story of a battle between buyers and sellers. The four prices — Open, High, Low, Close — reveal who won and by how much.

BULLISH CANDLE
HIGHHighest price reached — top of upper wick
CLOSEWhere price closed — top of body (green)
OPENWhere price opened — bottom of body
LOWLowest price reached — bottom of lower wick
Close > Open = Buyers won this candle. Green body means bullish momentum.
BEARISH CANDLE
HIGHHighest price reached — top of upper wick
OPENWhere price opened — top of body (red)
CLOSEWhere price closed — bottom of body
LOWLowest price reached — bottom of lower wick
Close < Open = Sellers won this candle. Red body means bearish momentum.

// lesson 03 · continuation patterns
BULL & BEAR FLAGS

Flags are among the most powerful continuation setups in futures trading. A strong move followed by a tight consolidation — then another push in the same direction.

BULL FLAG

A sharp move up (the pole), followed by a tight downward or sideways consolidation (the flag), then a breakout continuation upward.

  • Strong bullish pole — big green candles
  • Consolidation on low volume — small candles
  • Flag pulls back no more than 50% of pole
  • Enter on breakout above flag high
  • Target = pole height added to breakout
BEAR FLAG

A sharp move down (the pole), followed by a tight upward or sideways consolidation (the flag), then a breakdown continuation downward.

  • Strong bearish pole — big red candles
  • Consolidation on low volume — small candles
  • Flag retraces no more than 50% of pole
  • Enter on breakdown below flag low
  • Target = pole height subtracted from breakdown
⚠ EDUCATIONAL NOTE: Candlestick patterns are tools, not guarantees. Always use them in context — at key levels, with trend confirmation, and with a defined risk/reward. No pattern works 100% of the time.
