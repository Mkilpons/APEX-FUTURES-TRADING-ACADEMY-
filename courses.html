FAQ
Do I need any prior trading experience?+
No. The curriculum starts from absolute zero — what a futures contract is, how to read a chart, and how to set up your first paper trading account. Complete beginners finish Module 1 in a weekend.
How long before I can trade with real money?+
We recommend completing all 8 modules AND being consistently profitable on simulation for at least 30 consecutive trading days before going live. Most students hit this benchmark in 60–90 days. Rushing this step is the #1 mistake new traders make.
What markets does this cover?+
The core curriculum focuses on ES (S&P 500) and NQ (Nasdaq) futures — the two most liquid and beginner-friendly markets. The concepts taught apply directly to GC (Gold), CL (Crude Oil), and all other futures markets.
