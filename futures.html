FUTURES 101

Four things every new trader must understand before placing their first futures trade.

01

Leverage

Futures use margin — you control a large contract with a fraction of its value. A $1,200 MES margin controls a ~$220,000 position. This amplifies both gains AND losses equally. Respect it.

02

Tick Value

Every futures contract has a minimum price movement called a tick. For ES that's 0.25 points = $12.50. If price moves 10 points in your favor on one ES contract, you make $500. Against you — you lose $500.

03

Expiration

Futures contracts expire quarterly (March, June, September, December). Most traders roll to the next contract before expiry. Your platform will show the active front-month contract automatically.

04

Session Hours

Most equity futures trade nearly 24 hours. The most liquid window is the regular US session: 9:30am–4:00pm EST. The open (9:30–10:30am) and close (3:00–4:00pm) have the highest volume and volatility.

MICRO vs FULL

Should you start with micro contracts or full-size? The answer is almost always micro — here's why.

✓ Start Here

MICRO CONTRACTS

  • 1/10th the size of full contracts
  • MES margin ~$1,200 (vs ~$12,000 for ES)
  • Same chart, same setup, same execution
  • Lose less while you're learning
  • Build confidence without blowing up
  • Scale up gradually as you prove consistency
  • Available: MES, MNQ, MGC, MYM, MCL
Recommended for all beginners
When You're Ready

FULL CONTRACTS

  • 10x the size and profit potential
  • ES margin ~$12,000 per contract
  • One ES contract = 10 MES contracts
  • Higher margin requirements
  • Requires proven consistency on micro first
  • Better for prop firm accounts
  • Available: ES, NQ, GC, CL, YM, SI
⚠ RISK DISCLOSURE: Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss. Margin requirements can change at any time. The values shown are approximate and for educational purposes only. Always verify current margins with your broker. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose completely.
