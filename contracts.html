MICRO vs FULL

Should you start with micro contracts or full-size? The answer is almost always micro — here's exactly why.

✓ Start Here

MICRO CONTRACTS

  • 1/10th the size of full contracts
  • MES margin ~$1,200 vs ~$12,000 for ES
  • Same chart, same setup, same execution
  • Lose far less while you're still learning
  • Build confidence without blowing your account
  • Scale up gradually as you prove consistency
  • Available: MES, MNQ, MGC, MYM, MCL
  • Offered on all major platforms (Tradovate, NinjaTrader, etc.)
Recommended for all beginners
When You're Ready

FULL CONTRACTS

  • 10x the profit potential per contract
  • ES margin ~$12,000 per contract
  • One ES contract = exactly 10 MES contracts
  • Higher capital requirement per trade
  • Requires proven consistency on micro first
  • Standard for prop firm funded accounts
  • Available: ES, NQ, GC, CL, YM, SI, RTY
  • Lower commissions per dollar traded at scale

HOW FUTURES WORK

Four things every new trader must understand before placing their first futures trade. No shortcuts here.

01

Leverage

Futures use margin — you control a large contract with a fraction of its value. A $1,200 MES margin controls a ~$220,000 position. This amplifies both gains AND losses equally. Respect it at all times.

02

Tick Value

Every contract has a minimum price movement called a tick. For ES that's 0.25 points = $12.50. If price moves 10 points in your favor on one ES contract you make $500. Against you — you lose $500. Know this cold.

03

Expiration

Futures contracts expire quarterly — March, June, September, December. Most traders roll to the next contract before expiry. Your platform automatically shows the active front-month contract.

04

Session Hours

Most equity futures trade nearly 24 hours. The most liquid window is the regular US session: 9:30am–4:00pm EST. The open (9:30–10:30am) and close (3:00–4:00pm) carry the highest volume and volatility.

05

Margin Calls

If your account equity drops below the maintenance margin your broker will issue a margin call — requiring you to deposit more funds or close positions. Avoid this by always knowing your risk before entry.

06

Mark to Market

Unlike stocks, futures are settled daily. Profits and losses are credited or debited to your account at the end of each session. Your account balance reflects real-time P&L at all times during the trading day.

⚠ RISK DISCLOSURE: Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss. Margin requirements can change at any time. All values shown are approximate and for educational purposes only. Always verify current margins with your broker. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose completely.
